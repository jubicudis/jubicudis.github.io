# Violet — voice direction and audition protocol

This is the inventor's approved **voice direction**, not approval of any particular synthesized voice or published recording.

## Sound

- Clearly feminine, **light and airy**.
- **Soft and gentle** rather than shrill, exaggerated, cartoonish, whisper-only, or strongly robotic.
- A natural, comfortable pitch: **not excessively high**. Do not raise pitch artificially to imply femininity.
- Clearly understandable speech with warmth and the range needed to introduce technical subjects to children and adults.
- No accent was specified by the inventor; accents are audition comparisons, not a choice already made.

## Cost and licensing

- Investigate and prefer **free** licensed methods before paid services.
- Monthly, annual, or one-time fees may be considered if reasonable, but no paid plan is selected or authorized by this document.
- Kokoro-82M model and voice assets are Apache-2.0: https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
- The kokoro-onnx inference library is MIT licensed: https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx
- Each candidate recording is **audition-only** until actually listened to, reviewed and approved.

The auditions are generated by `tools/generate_voice_auditions.py` through
`.github/workflows/voice-auditions.yml` on the existing `main` branch. The script reads the **actual opening dialogue** from `site/opening-state.mjs`, generates the same words for every voice, verifies that real audio data exists, and uploads WAV files and a provenance manifest as a GitHub Actions artifact. Generation is not tied to visitor interaction or website deployment. Model weights are downloaded for CI only, not committed to the public website.

## Authority and synchronization requirements

- The presentation state owns **when and what** Violet communicates. The voice system owns only speech execution and audio observations.
- Requested playback is not successful playback; emit start/end/error/interrupted events only when they actually occur.
- Audio-clock or real phoneme timing must govern mouth synchronization, not a made-up oscillation presented as lip sync.
- A source change must be explicit. No silent switch from the approved voice to an arbitrary device voice following failure.
- Visitors may supply names or skip them. We must solve pronunciation of arbitrary names without switching to a visibly/audibly different persona or inventing what was spoken.
- Keep the currently accepted Structured Bloom A2 appearance and the opening experience unchanged until the selected audio is approved and propagation is implemented.

## Review

Listen to the **greeting** (real first-load phrase) and **opening** (greeting plus the skip-name welcome) for all candidates on an actual iPhone and another device. Compare feminine identity, airiness, natural pitch, warmth, softness, intelligibility, pacing, and odd pronunciation artifacts. The free direction is established; the final voice is deliberately **not** selected until the recording is accepted.
